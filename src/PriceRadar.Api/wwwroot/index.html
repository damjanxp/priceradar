How the register works

  1. Write down what the stall has: the title, the author if you can see it, and the asking price.
  2. PriceRadar finds the book among the listings it has collected, whatever the spelling or alphabet.
  3. It stamps a verdict: cheap at or below the cheapest quarter of recent prices, expensive above the dearest quarter, fair in between.
